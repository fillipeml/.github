# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith, say what you mean plainly, and
accept a correction when you are wrong. Disagreeing with a technical decision is welcome and is
most of what these repositories are for; being unpleasant about it is not.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind, insults, demeaning comments, or unwelcome attention. Publishing
somebody's private information. Sustained disruption. Anything that would make a reasonable
person decide not to open an issue here.

That applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, review comments, and anywhere else
connected to these repositories.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces, and when somebody is representing the project elsewhere.

Report a problem privately through the Security tab of the repository concerned, or through the
contact details on the profile page. Reports are read by me and handled privately; I will not
discuss a report with anyone who is not involved in it.

I will respond proportionately: a private word for a first lapse, removal of content that
breaches this document, and a block for anything repeated or severe. I will say what I have done
and why to the person affected.
